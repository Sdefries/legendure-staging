What a strong engagement looks like

  1. 01

    Clear scope

    Define responsibilities, skills, duration, working arrangements, and success criteria before sourcing or mobilizing resources.

  2. 02

    Transparent ownership

    Identify who directs the work, who manages delivery risk, and who approves changes.

  3. 03

    Useful reporting

    Agree on work products, checkpoints, and escalation paths so both parties can see progress.

Questions worth asking

Can we engage one person rather than a full team? +

Yes. Individual staff augmentation and fractional consulting are separate options from managed delivery engagements.

Can Legendure manage an entire workstream? +

That can be structured as a managed-team or project-based engagement when the scope, responsibilities, and delivery resources are agreed in advance.

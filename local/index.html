Common questions.

How fast will we see more calls? +

Missed-call text-back helps right away, because it saves calls you are already losing. Maps and search improvements usually take about 90 days to show and keep building after that.

Do we need a new website? +

Not always. If your site loads fast, works on phones and makes it easy to call or book, we keep it and improve it. If it needs replacing, a new site is included in Stay Busy and Full Schedule.

Are we locked into a long contract? +

Plans have a 6-month minimum, which is about how long Maps and search work takes to pay off. After that it's month to month. A 12-month plan lowers your monthly price.

What does it cost? +

Plans run $1,500, $2,750 or $3,950 a month. Ad spend is separate and paid directly to Google. The free check tells you which level fits your shop.

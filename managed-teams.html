Ownership is designed into the contract

Before an engagement begins, define the deliverables, management structure, assumptions, dependencies, decision rights, and acceptance criteria. Not every initiative needs a fixed price; the commercial model should match the uncertainty and degree of control over the work.

Questions worth asking

What's the difference between a managed team and staff augmentation? +

With staff augmentation, the client directs an individual contractor's work. In a managed engagement, delivery responsibilities and oversight are assigned at the team or workstream level.

Can we start with one workstream? +

Yes. A narrow initial scope can establish responsibilities and reduce risk before a larger engagement is considered.

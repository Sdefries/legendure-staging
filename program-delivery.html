A practical first engagement

Start with a focused delivery review: objectives, current plan, stakeholder map, dependencies, key risks, and immediate decisions. Use the findings to define whether ongoing fractional leadership, project management, or a managed workstream is warranted.

Yes. A subcontract or embedded leadership arrangement can be scoped around the firm's delivery responsibilities and client requirements.
